About Me

Hi, I'm Bohdan — Software Engineer at Talkable. During the day I write code that (mostly) does what it's supposed to; the rest of the time I still mostly write code, if I'm being honest with myself.

I like computers. Most of all I like watching an idea turn into something that actually works — and knowing which tool to reach for is half the craft. I enjoy a good mechanical keyboard, a strong cup of coffee, and the kind of late‑night refactor that definitely could have waited until morning but somehow never does.

This page is a small, cheerfully outdated tribute to the homepages of 2004 — back when everyone had one, nobody had heard of a "responsive layout," and a good marquee tag was the height of self‑expression.
